Trained in measurement rather than in health, which shapes the course: it asks
what a claim about your day is made of rather than which claims are true.

Present in person for weeks 3, 5, 7 and 11 — the four labs that ask students to
change something about their sleep, their eating or their caffeine. That is
deliberate. A course that sets those exercises should not run them by
correspondence.

**Ask me first** about anything that concerns your own safety in a protocol, or
any experiment you are uneasy about. You do not need to have a reason ready, and
asking to take the alternative protocol costs you nothing — not marks, not an
explanation.
