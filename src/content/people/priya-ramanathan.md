Background in instrumentation, which in this course mostly means arriving at
somebody's beautiful experimental design and asking how long a single reading
takes. If the honest answer is four minutes, the design is already dead and it is
better to know in week 2.

Marks the protocol portfolios. The thing most often lost there is not rigour but
legibility — a record that made complete sense to its author in March and is
unreadable to them by May.

**Ask me first** about your measure, your logging, and whether what you have
written down can actually be run by somebody else.
