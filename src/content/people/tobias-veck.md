Keeps the shared records: the pooled week 1 logs the class works from in week 9,
the anonymised sets that stand in for anyone taking an alternative protocol, and
the synthetic dataset used in week 11.

That last one is generated from noise and nothing else. It reliably produces two
or three correlations a reasonable person would believe, which is the entire
lesson of week 11 and the reason a student who should not be tracking their own
life learns it better rather than worse.

**Ask me first** about anything to do with the class data — what is pooled, what
is anonymised, and what never leaves your own notebook.
