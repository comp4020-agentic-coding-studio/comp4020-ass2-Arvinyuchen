A clinical trial assigns people to conditions at random, which is how it makes
everything other than the treatment average out. You cannot do this. You get one
life, in order, and it does whatever it was going to do.

[Confounding](/glossary/#confounding) is the consequence: something you were not
tracking moved at the same time as the thing you were, and you have no way to
tell which one produced the result.

## The list

Open your week 5 records. Write down everything that also changed during those
days. Not the plausible things — everything.

Weather. A deadline. Who you ate with. Whether you were annoyed with someone.
Daylight. A cold coming on. Money. A new season of something. Whether you were
avoiding a piece of work by doing this course's homework instead.

Ten minimum. Most people reach thirty and stop out of embarrassment.

Then rank them: for each, could it plausibly have produced the effect you
measured? You will find two or three that could have done the entire job on their
own, and the honest version of your week 5 conclusion has to name them.

## Why we chose the evening

The component this week is the social evening, because other people are the
confounder nobody controls for and everybody has. Your day is not a closed
system. It contains a housemate who came home in a mood, a friend who cancelled,
a sibling who called. None of that appears in a sleep tracker, and all of it
shows up in your data.

## The move that rescues this

You cannot randomise, but you can sometimes find a comparison that nature
arranged for you.

In 1854 John Snow was trying to show that cholera travelled in water rather than
air, against the obvious objection that poor neighbourhoods had both bad water
and everything else bad. So he found streets in south London where two different
water companies supplied neighbouring houses — one drawing from a polluted
stretch of the Thames, one from upstream. Same streets, same poverty, same air,
different water.

He had not assigned anybody to anything. He had found a place where the
assignment had already been made, for commercial reasons, by someone with no
interest in cholera.

That is the move to look for in your own life: two stretches of time, or two
parts of a week, where the thing you care about differs and most of the rest does
not. It is rarer than you want and better than nothing, and finding one is this
week's real exercise.
