Three phases. Measure the thing as it is. Take something away. Put it back.

That is a [withdrawal design](/glossary/#withdrawal-design), and it is the most
useful experiment available to a person working alone. Its logic is simple: if
the effect leaves when the thing leaves and returns when the thing returns, the
thing is a plausible cause. Coincidence has to work much harder to produce that
pattern twice.

## Why the third phase is the one that matters

Most people run two phases and stop. They remove breakfast, feel worse, conclude
breakfast matters, and go back to eating it, without measuring the going back.

But the second phase is when you were also getting used to a new routine, and
possibly sleeping badly, and possibly annoyed about the whole exercise. Putting
the thing back is what separates *breakfast* from *change*. If the effect does
not reverse, you learned something much more interesting than you were expecting.

So the return phase is assessable, and it is assessable specifically because it
is the phase everyone skips.

## About the self-experimenting tradition

You are joining an old and slightly alarming lineage. In 1984 Barry Marshall
could not persuade anyone that a bacterium caused stomach ulcers, so he drank a
culture of it, became ill within days, and eventually shared a Nobel Prize.

The lesson usually drawn is about courage. The lesson worth drawing is about
control: Marshall had one subject, no comparison, and no way of not knowing what
he had done. His experiment was decisive because the effect was enormous and
fast. Yours will not be, so you need the design to do work that drama cannot.

## The limits of this particular week

Read the boundary at the top of this page before you plan anything. Breakfast is
used here because it is a convenient, reversible, everyday variable that almost
everyone has an opinion about, not because this course has a view on whether
you should eat it.

If the removal phase starts to feel like an achievement rather than a
measurement, that is the signal to stop. Run the lamp instead. The method
transfers perfectly and nobody needs to be hungry to learn it.
