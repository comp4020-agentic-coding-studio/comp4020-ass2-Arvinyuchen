## You did not start this on a good day

Nobody takes up a sleep routine during a week they slept beautifully.

Nobody reorganises their day on a day that went well.

```notes
Ask the room: put a hand up if you have ever started something new on a good
day. Usually two or three hands, and they are worth asking about.
```

# Unusual measurements tend to be followed by ordinary ones

```notes
Now the name: regression to the mean. Galton, 1886, measuring the heights of
parents and their adult children — unusually tall parents had children closer
to the average than themselves.

Not a force. Not a tendency towards mediocrity. Arithmetic.
```

---

## So anything you begin at the bottom looks like it worked

Not sometimes. **Reliably** — and convincingly, from the inside, because you
genuinely did feel better afterwards.

You are not being fooled about how you felt.

You are being fooled about why.

```notes
This is the line to slow down on. The feeling is real. The causal story is what
is wrong. People get defensive here if you imply they imagined it.
```

---

## The flight instructors

Instructors noticed that praising a cadet after an exceptional landing made the
next one worse. Criticising one after a bad landing made the next one better.

They built a training philosophy on it.

An exceptional landing is followed by a more ordinary one. So is a terrible one.

```notes
Kahneman, Thinking Fast and Slow, chapter 17.

The sting: they spent years drawing a lesson about praise and criticism from a
fact about arithmetic, and the lesson they drew was to be harsher with people.

Pause after that. It usually lands.
```

---

## What this does to your own week 5 result

If you began your experiment on an unusual day, part of your result is the day
being unusual.

Go back to your week 1 baseline. Find your strangest day. Look at the three
days after it.

That is the size of the effect you would have claimed.

```notes
They will do this in the lab. Flag that it is uncomfortable for anyone whose
week 5 result was the thing they were most pleased with.
```

---

## Two things that help

**A control condition.** Something to compare against that also started at the
bottom.

**A pre-registration.** Written before you began, so the question cannot move
once you have seen the answer.

Neither removes the problem. Both make it visible.

```notes
Link back to week 6's pre-registration, which they have already submitted.
Point out they did the right thing before they knew why.
```
