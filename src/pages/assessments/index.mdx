# Assessment

Four pieces. They total 100%, and nothing is assessed before it has been taught —
both of which are checked when the site builds rather than promised here.

**You are marked on the quality of your reasoning and never on whether your day
improved.** That single rule shapes all four briefs. It is not modesty; it
follows from week 9. People start new routines on their worst days, and unusual
days are followed by ordinary ones, so almost any intervention appears to work.
Awarding marks for improvement would be awarding them for arithmetic.

So a carefully run experiment that found nothing scores well, and a confident
claim a design cannot support does not. Null results are reported at the same
length as positive ones.

The first deadline is the strictest and the earliest: a pre-registration cannot
be extended once you have started collecting data. See the
[policies](/policies/) for the rest.
