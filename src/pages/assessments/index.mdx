Four pieces totalling 100%, none of them assessed before it has been taught.

**You are marked on the quality of your reasoning and never on whether your day
improved.** That follows from week 9: people start new routines on their worst
days, and unusual days are followed by ordinary ones, so almost any intervention
appears to work. Marks for improvement would be marks for arithmetic.

So a careful experiment that found nothing scores well, and a confident claim a
design cannot support does not. Null results are reported at the same length as
positive ones.

The earliest deadline is also the strictest: a pre-registration cannot be
extended once you have started collecting data. The
[policies](/policies/) cover the rest.
