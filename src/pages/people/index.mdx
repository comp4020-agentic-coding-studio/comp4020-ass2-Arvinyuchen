# People

Three of us. Each entry ends with what to ask that person about first, because
the escalation path is the part of a staff page anybody actually needs.

The convenor is listed on four labs and no others: weeks 3, 5, 7 and 11, the
ones that ask you to change something about your sleep, your eating or your
caffeine. A course that sets those exercises should not run them by
correspondence.

For anything concerning your own safety in a protocol, or an experiment you are
uneasy about, go to the convenor directly. You do not need to have a reason
ready.
