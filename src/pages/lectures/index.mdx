# Lectures

Three lectures, not twelve. The labs are where the course happens — you arrive
with a record of your own day and leave with a result about it — and a lecture
is only scheduled where something genuinely needs telling rather than doing.

Week 1 takes apart four instructions you have already been given. Week 6 traces
the twenty-five minute work block back to one student and one kitchen timer, and
asks what it means that the world adopted somebody's n of 1. Week 9 is the
uncomfortable one, and the only hour of the course that will change how you read
every health claim you meet afterwards.
