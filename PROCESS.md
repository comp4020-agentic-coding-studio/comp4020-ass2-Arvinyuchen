# Process overview

## What I built

**SLOP1638: How to Make a Day.** A twelve-week course on running experiments on
yourself, on one argument: the instructions you are given about your day are
statements about a population, handed over as instructions about your Tuesday.

## What I decided a good course looks like

Assignment 1 scored 95 for the artefact and 62 for process. My harness there ran
to 7,648 words with no rule about content, factuality or audience, so those
decisions left no trace.

So the design came first, in writing: the premise, the scope I was refusing, the
twelve weeks keyed on distinct methods, the unit one day rather than a habit
([`dd4405e`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/dd4405e)).

## What went into the harness

Six rules, each a course value rather than a coding standard
([`9be7c3c`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/9be7c3c)),
and then the checks, committed **failing**, before any course content existed
([`7291c68`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/7291c68)).
The ordering is the evidence.

Two obligations went into the content schema, not `spec/`: the deploy job does
not depend on the check job, and would ship a week with no stopping rule
([`8f8fb3f`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/8f8fb3f)).

Content bent the harness four times. My claim schema demanded a sample size from
every claim, but "the 10,000-step target began as a product name" is history,
not an effect
([`4567725`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/4567725)).
Week 1 argues for nothing and owes no receipts, so rule 2 gained a named
exemption list, not an emptiable field, and a test that a listed week carries
none
([`5b48460`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/5b48460)).
A run together bug shipped five times, so the fifth bought a check over the
built pages: four live, two on the home page
([`dfd5a32`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/dfd5a32)).
The paragraph floor reached the briefs; the eight it caught were rewritten by
argument, not padded
([`4f47077`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/4f47077)).

## Keeping it factual

A course about reading evidence honestly made me a worse customer for what the
agent handed back: every number checked against the source, and cut rather than
softened. A lecture drafted *about provenance* carried three details no primary
source supports, already propagated into its lab
([`0bb242a`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/0bb242a)).

## Measurement instead of judgement

A five step ramp died at deltaE 5 against a floor of 15
([`07b70a8`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/07b70a8));
a four step ring ramp died at 11, and the fix was one hue with the arcs labelled
directly
([`e592172`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/e592172)).
The export button's two states measured 2.3:1 against each other while reading
respectably against the page, so the author saw the problem where the numbers
could not
([`52349ec`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/52349ec)).
One trap: a dark mode failure that was an artefact of forcing a colour scheme
onto a rendered document
([`e36c082`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/e36c082)).

## What I removed

An Apple Health sleep reader was built on a synthetic fixture, shipped, and
raised week 1 to medium risk with the three safety fields the schema demanded
([`374c6c5`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/374c6c5)).
The author decided it was not useful, so it all came out, risk change and week
3's rewording included
([`5854c29`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/5854c29)).

## How I knew, and what it cannot see

Twenty-one deliberate mutations. In Assignment 1 six of twelve left my suite
green; this time every one was caught, and the sweep found a flaw in my method
([`d50cc53...b765359`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/compare/d50cc53...b765359)).

The misses are the honest part. At 390px the claims table pushed the page
sideways
([`1d3c2fa`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/1d3c2fa)).
An effect claim can still be relabelled as history; the check for that fires on
this course's own legitimate history, so I abandoned it
([`b765359`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/b765359)).
The day sheet's drag is verified by reading, not by dragging
([`168aa8a`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Arvinyuchen/commit/168aa8a)),
and the axe pass, reading built HTML with scripts off, has never seen it
working. An agent also reported instructions the author never sent, caught
because the repo contradicted it.
